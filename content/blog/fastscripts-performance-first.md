At [FastScripts](https://fastscripts.tebex.io), I care about the boring metrics that players feel immediately: hitch warnings, load times, and config footguns.

Current priorities:

- reduce unnecessary entity churn
- tighten defaults so new installs "just work"
- keep changelogs honest and short

A fast script that breaks configs is not fast. It is unfinished.
