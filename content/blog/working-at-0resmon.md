Studio environments move differently from freelance.

At [0Resmon](https://0resmon.com) the pace forced clearer communication: scope, owners, deadlines, and what "done" means for a resource that thousands of servers might install.

What stuck with me:

- document assumptions before they become bugs
- ship smaller increments that can be tested
- treat support feedback as product input, not noise

Those habits now shape how I run CodeRanch and UiForge.
