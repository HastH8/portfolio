Roleplay UIs fail in the same ways as bad product dashboards: too many labels, unclear hierarchy, and zero feedback when something saves.

For [UiForge](https://uiforge.tebex.io) packs I obsess over:

- contrast that works on dark RP atmospheres
- buttons that look pressable without screaming "game menu"
- error states that tell the player the next step

Pretty CSS is table stakes. Usable NUI is the craft.
